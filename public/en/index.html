Hello, we are Haraido Yukiki.

Get your hands in the soil.
Meet new people.
Help a place grow.

Set in the rice paddies and fields of Miyukitsuji in Hashimoto, Wakayama, this is a project where people come together to work, learn and spend time close to nature and the land.

Join in with the farm work. Help build the place. Support us from afar.
We are thinking about different ways to take part, so you can find one that fits your life.

What is it?

Working side by side,
building connections bit by bit.

What matters to us is spending time with nature and the land, and doing things together with other people. Through the work in the fields and the things we notice along the way, we hope to create a place where people learn and try out new ideas.

We are still in the early days, so we are also looking for founding members to help lay the groundwork. That said, nobody has to take on organising. You might just come along once, or lend a hand now and then, and it is perfectly fine for the way you take part to change over time.

On the farm

Come for the day on a work day.

We will let you know about days when we need extra hands, such as rice planting or the harvest. Coming for just the morning or just the afternoon is absolutely fine.

What you'll be doing

  • Joining the big seasonal jobs, such as planting and harvesting rice, cutting wheat and gathering beans.
  • Helping with everyday upkeep, such as weeding and keeping the water channels clear.
  • This autumn we are sowing wheat and beans, and around June next year we plan to plant rice where the wheat has been.
  • Over the winter, visits are mainly day trips. The days you come along are arranged with the organiser.
A word you'll see on our pages

Ichiji ryugaku (day visit)

Coming to the site just for the key work days, as a day trip. Once you are used to it, you can also come along on a regular basis.

Build it together

Help get the place and the project off the ground.

For those who want to do more than join a ready-made experience, and would like a say in what we do next.

Some ways you could help

  • Right now we are getting the site ready for visitors. We plan to put up a small greenhouse and a rainwater tank on days when everyone gets together.
  • It is not only about farming: you can start with whatever you can bring, whether that is craft skills, ideas or people you know.
  • Bringing things you no longer use, such as a table and chairs, a tent or sleeping bag, or tools like sickles and hoes, is a real help too.
  • How much you do and how often is something to talk through with the organiser.

Support from afar

Still connected, even when you can't be there.

FAQ

Questions we often get
from newcomers.

Can I take part with no farming experience?

Yes. The project is designed so that people without experience can get used to things gradually. On work days, you will be working alongside the organiser and people who have been involved for a while.

Do I have to come every time?

No. Come when you can, and take a break when you can't. Please take part at your own pace.

If I register, do I have to take part or give support?

No. Registering on "Yukiki Fan" is just so that you receive plans and updates. It does not commit you to taking part or giving support.

What about insurance?

For now, we expect everyone to arrange their own cover (for example, health insurance and personal liability insurance). Please check the details with the organiser.

What should I wear and bring?

Clothes and shoes you don't mind getting dirty are the basics. The work varies from day to day, so we will let you know what to bring in the details for each work day.

Do I get any of the harvest?

If things grow well, we plan to share some of the harvest. To be honest, though, as we are only just starting, we don't yet know how much we will get in the first year.

Where we are / ABOUT US

Let's give it a go,
here, together.

Haraido Yukiki is a project set in the rice paddies and fields of Miyukitsuji in Hashimoto, Wakayama, where we work together close to nature and the land.

Keep living your city life, and come out to the fields every now and then. Get your hands busy, watch the seasons change, and share what you notice. That is the kind of involvement we hope to grow, little by little.

Location
Miyukitsuji, Hashimoto, Wakayama Prefecture, Japan
Getting here
About a 3-minute walk from Miyukitsuji Station on the Nankai Koya Line
Language
The sign-up page and the organiser's guide are in Japanese
What matters to us

You don't need to understand everything about the project from day one. Start by seeing what it is like. How deeply you get involved, and at what pace, is up to you.

We keep a record of the work we do together, the things we try and the things we are unsure about. We hope these records will help the people who join after us.
